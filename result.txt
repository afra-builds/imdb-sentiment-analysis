# IMDb Sentiment Analysis — Results

**Dataset:** IMDb 50K Movie Reviews  
**Dataset Source:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/lakshmi25npathi/imdb-dataset-of-50k-movie-reviews

**Data after cleaning:** 49,582 reviews  
**Training reviews:** 39,665  
**Testing reviews:** 9,917  

**Model:** Multinomial Naive Bayes  
**Text Vectorization:** CountVectorizer  
**Maximum Features:** 20,000  
**Test Accuracy:** 85.19%

**Incorrect Predictions:** 1,469

**Prediction Function Tests:**
1. Positive review → positive
2. Negative review → negative
3. Empty review → Please enter a review!

**Limitations:** The model may misclassify reviews containing sarcasm, mixed opinions, or complex language. The accuracy is based on this particular test split.
